  • Small Business Saturday 2026 is Saturday, November 28, the day after Black Friday. That leaves about two weeks to prepare.
  • In the 2026 American Express Shop Small Impact Study, 88% of consumers said they are likely to shop at small businesses this holiday season, the highest level since 2012.

The best Small Business Saturday marketing ideas are simple: one clear reason to visit on November 28, a way for happy customers to tell their friends, and enough visibility in the two weeks before the day that local shoppers know you are part of it. Small Business Saturday was founded by American Express in 2010 and has been cosponsored by the U.S. Small Business Administration since 2011, according to the SBA's Small Business Saturday page. This guide covers what the newest shopper data says, ten practical promotion ideas, and a day-by-day plan you can start today.

What the 2026 Shop Small data tells small businesses

American Express published its 2026 Shop Small Impact Study on November 12, 2026. It combines an online survey of 1,008 U.S. adults (fielded September 9-11, 2026) with a survey of 503 owners and managers of small businesses that have a physical location. Because the study is commissioned by the company behind the event, read it as a measure of intent, not a sales forecast. Still, several findings are useful when you plan a promotion:

  • Intent is high. 88% of consumers agreed they are likely to shop at small businesses this holiday season, the highest level since 2012.
  • People want gifts that feel personal. 72% said they shop small to find memorable gifts, and 70% said they enjoy discovering new small businesses during the holidays.
  • Discovery runs on word of mouth. Recommendations from friends and family were the top way consumers choose small businesses (65%), followed by customer reviews (45%) and social media posts (39%).
  • Owners are trying new things. 96% of owners said they are using new strategies to prepare for the season, and 90% expect to use AI tools during the holidays, including for marketing.
  • The relationship matters more than the day. 80% of owners said Small Business Saturday can help build customer relationships that extend beyond the holiday season.

Intent does not always turn into spending. Consumers reported spending an estimated $18 billion at small retailers and restaurants on Small Business Saturday 2025, down from $22 billion the year before, according to American Express figures reported by Forbes. The lesson for 2026: goodwill gets people interested, but a specific, well-promoted offer is what gets them through the door.

10 Small Business Saturday marketing ideas you can use this year

You don't need all ten. Pick two or three that fit your business and do them well.

Offers and in-store events

  1. A Saturday-only offer. Give people a reason to come on November 28 rather than "sometime this season": a gift with purchase, a bundle price, or a discount on one featured category. Put the end time in every message.
  2. Early-bird or first-50 perks. A small gift for the first customers of the day creates urgency without a deep discount.
  3. A gift guide built from your products. Group items by recipient or price ("gifts under $25", "for the cook"). This matches the 72% who shop small for memorable gifts, and the guide works as a web page, a printed handout and a social post.
  4. A neighborhood crawl. Team up with nearby shops on a shared map, a stamp card or a joint raffle. Each business brings its own customers, and everyone gets new ones.
  5. Gift cards with a bonus. For example, a $10 bonus card with every $50 gift card, redeemable in January. You get sales now and a reason for customers to come back after the holidays.

Word of mouth and online visibility

  1. A refer-a-friend reason. Since recommendations are the top discovery source, make them easy: "Bring a friend who's new to us and you both get a free gift."
  2. Ask happy customers for reviews now. Reviews were the second most common discovery source. A short request after a purchase this week can add fresh reviews before shoppers start searching.
  3. Update your listings and holiday hours. Shoppers check opening hours before they leave home. Google lets you add special hours for holidays and one-off events in your Google Business Profile; do the same on your website and social profiles.
  4. Post a short countdown on social media. Show the products, the people behind the counter and the offer. Three or four posts over two weeks are enough; consistency matters more than volume.
  5. Run a simple banner ad that points to your offer. A display ad on relevant websites puts your offer in front of people who don't follow you yet. Link it to a page about the Saturday offer, not your home page, and tag the link so you can measure it.

Free Small Business Saturday tools and materials

Before you design anything from scratch, check the free resources:

  • American Express Shop Small materials. The Amex Shop Small merchant page offers free Small Business Saturday marketing materials, such as signage, printable posters and ready-made social posts. Amex says you do not need to be a Card Member or accept American Express Cards to get the downloadable materials. Qualifying businesses that accept the card can also be recommended on the Shop Small Map that shoppers use to find participating businesses; check the eligibility rules on the page.
  • SBA resources. The SBA page links to the free materials and to a 2026 Small Business Saturday social media guide and graphics.
  • Your local chamber or main street group. Many organize shared events, maps or "passport" programs.

A two-week plan: how to prepare for Small Business Saturday

This week (November 13-20)

  • Choose your one main offer and write it in one sentence.
  • Download the free Shop Small materials and order any printed signs.
  • Create a landing page or post about the offer and build your tagged links.
  • Design and submit your banner ad so it is reviewed and live before Thanksgiving week.
  • Ask recent customers for reviews.

Thanksgiving week (November 21-27)

  • Add special hours for Thanksgiving, Black Friday and Saturday to your Google Business Profile and website.
  • Send a teaser email or text to your customers.
  • Post your gift guide and a behind-the-scenes photo or video.

Small Business Saturday (November 28)

  • Post a "we're open" update in the morning with the offer and hours.
  • Hand every customer something to take home: a bounce-back coupon for December or January, or a referral card.
  • Collect email sign-ups at checkout so you can thank customers and invite them back.

After the weekend

  • Send a thank-you message with a reason to return before Christmas.
  • Review your analytics and in-store notes. Keep what worked for your last-minute holiday marketing push in December.

Frequently asked questions

When is Small Business Saturday 2026?

Saturday, November 28, 2026, the day after Black Friday and two days before Cyber Monday. The SBA and American Express both list this date.

How do I promote my business for Small Business Saturday?

Choose one clear offer, update your hours and listings, use the free Shop Small marketing materials, ask happy customers for reviews and referrals, post a short countdown on social media, and consider a low-cost ad that links to a page about your offer.

Can online businesses take part in Small Business Saturday?

Yes. The day is best known for local shops and restaurants, but online sellers can run Saturday offers too. American Express says online businesses can get its free digital marketing materials, and qualifying online merchants that accept the card can appear in the online directory section of its Shop Small Map.

Are Small Business Saturday marketing materials free?

Yes. American Express offers free Small Business Saturday marketing materials, such as signs, printable posters and social media posts, and says you don't need to accept American Express Cards to download them. The SBA also links to a free social media guide and graphics.

How far ahead should I plan Small Business Saturday ads?

Ideally two to four weeks ahead, so your design, landing page and tracked links are ready and any ad review is finished before Thanksgiving week. If you are starting now, submit your ad this week.
