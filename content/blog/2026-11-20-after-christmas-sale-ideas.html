Key takeaways

  • The six days after Christmas are busy, not dead. Adobe found that 1 in every 7 online holiday returns in 2025 happened between December 26 and December 31.
  • Retailers expected 17% of holiday sales to be returned in 2025, according to the National Retail Federation. Every return is a customer you can sell to again.

The best after Christmas sale ideas give people a reason to come back between December 26 and December 31: an easy exchange, a bonus for spending a gift card, a clear clearance offer or a fresh-start product for the new year. Shoppers are off work, holding gift cards and returning gifts, so the week is a real selling window for small businesses. This guide shows what the data says about that week, eight sale ideas you can set up now, a day-by-day plan for 2026 and how to run banner ads that support it.

Why the week after Christmas is worth advertising

Many small businesses stop marketing on December 24. That leaves a gap at a time when customers are still active. Three sets of numbers explain why.

Returns bring customers back

Adobe's report on the 2025 holiday shopping season, published January 7, 2026, found that online returns rose 4.7% in the days after Christmas (December 26 to 31), and "1 out of every 7 returns this season happened during this time period." Adobe also found that 38.8% of returns were made on a mobile device.

The National Retail Federation's 2025 Retail Returns Landscape said retailers expected 17% of holiday sales to be returned, "consistent with previous years." The same report found that 76% of consumers are more likely to choose a return option that gives an instant refund or exchange, and 71% are less likely to shop again with a retailer after a poor returns experience. A smooth exchange is marketing in its own right.

Gift cards are waiting to be spent

A Bankrate survey conducted by YouGov in August 2024 found that 43% of US adults had at least one unused gift card, gift voucher or store credit. The average unused amount was $244 per person and the median was $100. That data is from 2024, but the habit it shows is useful: many people hold value they have not spent, and a nudge can bring them in.

Spending stays online and on phones

Adobe forecasts a record $275.1 billion in US online holiday spending for November 1 to December 31, 2026, up 6.7% from 2025. It expects mobile to drive 57.4% of online spending for the season and expects discounts of "up to 10-15% throughout December." Your post-holiday ads will mostly be seen on phones, and your offer does not need to beat Black Friday prices to be competitive.

8 after Christmas sale ideas for small businesses

Pick one or two ideas that fit your business. A single clear offer works better in a banner than a list of five.

  1. Exchange bonus. Offer a small extra, such as 10% off an add-on item, when a customer exchanges a gift. It turns a return into a sale. Example banner text: "Exchanging a gift? Get 10% off anything you add."
  2. Gift card spend bonus. Reward people who use a gift card this week, for example "Spend $50 with a gift card, get $10 off." Set a clear end date, such as December 31.
  3. Holiday clearance. Clear seasonal stock, decorations and wrapping before January. Show the discount and the category in the banner, not just the word "Sale."
  4. "Gift to yourself" offers. Once the gift-giving is over, promote your best sellers to people shopping for themselves, with a simple, honest discount.
  5. New Year's Eve offers. Restaurants, bakeries, liquor stores, party suppliers and salons can promote reservations, platters, outfits or appointments for December 31.
  6. Fresh-start bundles. Fitness, organizing, planners, cleaning and home services all fit the new-year mood. A "start January ready" bundle bridges into your January marketing.
  7. Prepaid bookings for January. Service businesses can sell January appointments, classes or memberships at a small discount if paid before December 31.
  8. Thank-you offer for holiday customers. Send customers who bought in December a return-visit code valid through early January. It costs little and rewards loyalty.

How to write post-holiday banner ads that get clicks

A banner has a second or two to make its point. Keep it simple.

  • Lead with the offer. "Gift card? Get $10 off $50" is clearer than "Holiday savings event."
  • Show the end date. "Ends Dec 31" gives a real reason to act now. Only use deadlines you will keep.
  • Use one call to action. "Shop clearance" or "Book now," not both.
  • Design for phones. With mobile driving most online spending, use large text and high contrast. Our banner ad sizes guide shows which formats work best on small screens.
  • Drop the Christmas imagery. After December 25, red-and-green themes look dated. Winter, clearance or new-year visuals fit better.

Send every banner to a page that matches it. If the ad says "Gift card bonus," the page should explain the bonus at the top. Our guide to building a banner ad landing page covers what to include.

Make returns and exchanges part of your marketing

Returns are part of holiday retail, so plan for them instead of hoping they do not happen. The NRF numbers above show that customers care about speed and simplicity, and that a bad experience costs future sales. Some low-cost steps:

  • Put your return and exchange policy, including any extended holiday window, in a short, easy-to-find section of your site.
  • Train staff to offer an exchange or store credit first, with a small bonus, before processing a refund.
  • Place your best add-on items near the returns counter or on the online returns confirmation page.
  • Follow up with customers who exchanged an item and give them a reason to visit again in January.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Going silent from December 25 to January 2.
  • Leaving Black Friday or Christmas banners running after the holiday.
  • Offering several discounts at once, so no single message is clear.
  • Hiding your returns policy or your holiday hours.
  • Promising deadlines or stock you cannot honor.

Frequently asked questions

Do people really shop the week after Christmas?

Yes. Many people are off work, returning gifts and holding gift cards. Adobe found that online returns rose 4.7% from December 26 to 31, 2025, and that 1 in 7 holiday-season returns happened in those days. Each return is a chance to make another sale.

What should I put on sale after Christmas?

Seasonal stock you need to clear, popular items people wanted but did not receive, and products or services that fit New Year's Eve or new-year goals. Pick one main offer for your ads so the message stays clear.

How long should an after-Christmas sale last?

Most small businesses run it from December 26 to December 31, then switch to January offers. A short, fixed window with a stated end date gives shoppers a reason to act and keeps your margins under control.

When should I set up post-holiday ads?

Before Christmas. Write the offer, design the banner and book the placement in mid-December so it is approved and live by December 26, when returns and gift card spending begin.

How do I get people to spend their gift cards with me?

Give them a reason and a deadline, such as a bonus discount when they spend a set amount with a gift card before December 31. Mention it in your banners, emails, social posts and at checkout.
