ChatGPT ads for small business are now a real option: since May 2026, US businesses can sign up for OpenAI's beta self-serve Ads Manager, set a budget and bid, and pay per click or per thousand impressions to appear below ChatGPT's answers. The ads reach adult users on the Free and Go plans and are matched to the topic of the conversation. They are promising for businesses whose customers ask ChatGPT for recommendations, but the platform is young, prices are still settling and measurement depends on setting up tracking yourself. This guide explains how the ads work, what is known about costs, and how to run a small, controlled test.

How ChatGPT ads work

OpenAI announced plans to test ads in January 2026 and started showing them on February 9. According to Search Engine Journal's report on the launch, the test went live "for logged-in adult users in the U.S." on the Free plan and the Go plan, which costs $8 a month. Accounts identified as under 18 don't get ads.

Where the ads appear

Ads show at the bottom of a ChatGPT response, visually separated from the answer and labeled as sponsored. Users can dismiss an ad, see why it appeared, turn off ad personalization or clear ad-related data. Free users can also opt out of ads in exchange for fewer free messages a day.

How ads are matched to people

Matching is mainly contextual. CBS News quoted OpenAI as saying it shows ads by "matching ads submitted by advertisers with the topic of your conversation." OpenAI also says past chats and past interactions with ads can be used. Some topics are off-limits: OpenAI said it would not show ads in conversations about health, mental health or politics.

What advertisers can and can't see

Advertisers don't get chat transcripts or personal details. In its May 5, 2026 announcement, OpenAI said ads are shown "without sharing conversations or personal details with advertisers," and that advertisers receive "aggregated performance insights." In practice, that means you see totals such as impressions and clicks, not who clicked or what they asked.

How to advertise on ChatGPT

There are two ways to buy. Large brands can work through partners, including agencies Dentsu, Omnicom, Publicis and WPP and ad-tech companies such as Adobe, Criteo, Kargo, Pacvue and StackAdapt. Small businesses will more likely use the self-serve route.

OpenAI's beta Ads Manager lets an advertiser register, add payment details, set budgets, bids and pacing, upload ads, and launch and manage campaigns. OpenAI says the tool is for "companies of all sizes, from SMBs and startups to global brands," and that access is opening gradually. The Decoder reported that the beta launched for US advertisers in early May 2026.

A typical setup looks like this:

  1. Apply for Ads Manager access through OpenAI's sign-up page and add a payment method.
  2. Choose a bidding model: cost per click (CPC), where you pay only when someone clicks, or CPM, where you pay per thousand times your ad is shown.
  3. Set a total budget and pacing so spending can't run past what you planned.
  4. Write and upload your ad, with a landing page that matches it.
  5. Install measurement. OpenAI offers a pixel and a Conversions API so you can attribute purchases, leads and sign-ups to ad clicks.

Because the product is in beta, screens, options and eligibility rules may change. Check OpenAI's current help pages before you commit money.

What do ChatGPT ads cost?

There is no public rate card, so be wary of any article that quotes a fixed price. What has been reported:

StageWhat was reportedSource
Initial test (February 2026)Early advertisers faced a minimum commitment of $200,000; media buyers cited about $60 per 1,000 viewsSearch Engine Journal, citing Adweek and Digiday
Later in the pilotMinimum reduced to $50,000The Decoder
Self-serve beta (May 2026)CPC bidding added next to CPM; The Decoder reported "the $50,000 minimum budget is gone"OpenAI; The Decoder

Two points matter for a small budget. First, the $60 CPM figure came from the earliest, invitation-only phase and is not a current price. Second, with auction-style bidding, your actual cost depends on how many other businesses want the same conversations. For a local plumber that may be modest; for a busy category like insurance or software it may not be. Start with CPC bidding if you want to pay only for visits, and set a hard total budget.

Are ChatGPT ads worth it for a small business?

It depends on whether your customers already use ChatGPT to make the kind of decision you sell into. A large audience exists: Search Engine Journal, citing CNBC, put ChatGPT at about 800 million weekly users at the time of the launch. But only Free and Go users see ads, ads are excluded from some topics, and nobody outside OpenAI has published reliable small-business benchmarks yet.

Good candidates

  • Businesses people research before buying, such as home services, software tools, travel, courses and specialty retail.
  • Offers that are easy to explain in a sentence and act on from a phone.
  • Owners who are comfortable installing a pixel or working with someone who can.

Probably wait

  • Health and wellness businesses, since health conversations don't carry ads.
  • Businesses with a very small total ad budget that can't afford a test that teaches them little.
  • Anyone who needs predictable monthly costs above all else.

A low-risk plan to test ChatGPT ads

New ad platforms reward careful testers and punish people who spend first and measure later. A simple four-week plan:

  1. Pick one offer and one landing page. Keep the test clean so the results mean something.
  2. Set a fixed test budget you would be comfortable losing, and use CPC bidding so you pay only for clicks.
  3. Tag the link with UTM parameters, for example utm_source=chatgpt&utm_medium=cpc&utm_campaign=winter-test, so visits show up clearly in your own analytics. Our guide to UTM parameters shows how.
  4. Run a comparison channel at the same time, such as a search campaign or a flat-rate banner, with its own UTM tags.
  5. Judge on cost per lead or sale, not on clicks. Check conversions in your own analytics, not only in the platform's report.

After four weeks you will know whether ChatGPT brings customers at a price that works for you. If it does, scale slowly. If it doesn't, you have lost a small, known amount.

Frequently asked questions

Can small businesses advertise on ChatGPT?

Yes, in the US. In May 2026 OpenAI opened a beta self-serve Ads Manager that lets businesses register, set budgets and bids, upload ads and manage campaigns. Access has been rolling out gradually, so some businesses may wait before they can launch.

How much do ChatGPT ads cost?

There is no published price list. Ads are bought by auction with cost-per-click or CPM bidding. During the first test, buyers reported about $60 per 1,000 views and a $200,000 minimum commitment, but those minimums were later removed for self-serve buyers, and current prices depend on competition for each topic.

Who sees ads in ChatGPT?

Logged-in adult users in the US on the Free and Go plans. Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise and Education accounts don't see ads, and OpenAI says it doesn't show ads in conversations about health, mental health or politics.

Do advertisers see what people type into ChatGPT?

No. OpenAI says it does not share conversations or personal details with advertisers. Advertisers get aggregated results such as impressions and clicks, plus conversions if they install OpenAI's pixel or Conversions API.
